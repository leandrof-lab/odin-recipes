Burger Recipe

Description

A burger is a sandwich made with a cooked meat or plant-based patty inside a sliced bread bun. It is often topped with ingredients like cheese, lettuce, tomato, onion, pickles, and various sauces as ketchup of mayonnaise. Burgers are usually served hot and are popular in fast food and casual dining.

Ingredients

  • Burger buns
  • Ground beef
  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • Cheese slice
  • Lettuce
  • Tomato
  • Onion
  • Pickles
  • Ketchup
  • Mayonnaise
  • Mustard

Steps

  1. Season the ground beef with salt and peppper and form burger patties.
  2. Preheat a pan or grill oven medium-high heat.
  3. Cook the patties on each side until browned and cooked to your liking.
  4. Place cheese on the patties near the end of cooking to melt.
  5. Toast the burger buns lightly on the pan or grill.
  6. Spred sauces (ketchup, mayonnaise, mustard) on the buns.
  7. Add lettuce, tomato, onion, and pickles to the bottom bun.
  8. Place the patty with melted cheese on top.
  9. Cover with the top bun, serve, and enjoy.
