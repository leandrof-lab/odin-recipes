Cokkie Recipe

Description

A cookie is a small, sweet baked treat made from dough that typically includes flour, sugar, butter, and eggs. Cookies can be soft or crunchy and often contain extras like chocolate chips, nuts, or dried fruit.

Ingredients

  • Flour
  • Sugar
  • Butter
  • Eggs
  • Baking powder or baking soda
  • Salt
  • Vanilla extract
  • Chocolate chips (or other extras)

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to about 180° C.
  2. Mix soft butter and sugar until creamy.
  3. Add eggs and vanilla extract and mix well.
  4. Stir in flour, baking powder (or baking soda), and salt to form a dough.
  5. Fold in chocolate chips (or other extras).
  6. Place small portions of dough on a baking tray with parchment paper.
  7. Bake for about 8-12 minutes, until the edges are lightly golden.
  8. Let the cookies cool on a rack before serving.
