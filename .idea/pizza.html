Pizza Recipe

Description

A pizza is a round, flat bread topped with tomato sauce, melted cheese, and various ingredients like vegetables,meat or seafood. It is baked in an oven until the crust is crispy and the cheese is hot and bubbly. Pizza is often shared with friends or family and is enjoyed both as a quick snack and a full meal.

Ingredients

  • Pizza dough
  • Tomato sauce (or crushed tomatoes)
  • Fresh mozzarella cheese
  • Fresh basil leaves
  • Olive oil
  • Salt
  • (Optional) A little dried oregano or fresh tomato slices

Steps

  1. Prepare the dough and let it rise.
  2. Preheat the oven to a high temperature.
  3. Shape the dough into a round pizza base.
  4. Spread tomato sauce on the dough.
  5. Add mozzarella cheese and a little olive oil.
  6. Bake the pizza until the crust is golden and the cheese is melted
  7. Add fresh basil, slice, and serve.
